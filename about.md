I'm Max — a VFX artist based in Australia. By day I work in visual effects; the rest of the time I'm building things, breaking things, and occasionally writing about both.

## What I'm into

I ride motorcycles and chase big adventures on two wheels whenever I can. Between rides I'm usually tinkering with electronics, designing parts in CAD, or running yet another 3D print. I play video games, travel as much as possible, and have a general obsession with anything that moves — whether it's under sail, powered by an engine, or something weird that probably shouldn't work but does.

## Projects

This blog is where I document the builds and the things I learn along the way. Here's one example:

- **[Overhead Tracker](https://overheadtracker.com)** — Real-time aircraft tracking with a web app, Raspberry Pi proxy, and ESP32 hardware display. Born from living under a flight path and wanting to know what's flying over.
